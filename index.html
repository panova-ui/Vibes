Why Penne Rigate Beats Smooth Pasta

*Penne Rigate* features exterior ridged grooves designed to trap dense, velvety sauces. Smooth (*Penne Lisce*) causes heavy cream to slide right off into the bottom of the bowl.

🐟

Fresh Atlantic vs. Hot-Smoked Salmon

Searing fresh salmon cubes yields juicy, flakey tenderness with sweet oceanic flavor. Hot-smoked salmon brings deep wood-smoked complexity. Mixing 80% fresh + 20% smoked yields perfection!

Flavor Matrix Tip →
💧

The Liquid Gold (Pasta Water Emulsion)

Never discard all pasta boiling water! Starches dissolved in the water act as a natural emulsifier, binding heavy cream fat and olive oil into a glossy, non-greasy sauce.

Omega-3 Superfood Powerhouse

Salmon is loaded with EPA & DHA Omega-3 fatty acids, proven to boost cardiovascular health and brain function. Gentle sear preserves these heat-sensitive essential oils.

Sommelier Wine Pairing

Pair with high-acid crisp whites like **Pinot Grigio**, **Sauvignon Blanc**, or **Dry Riesling**. The citrusy acidity cuts straight through rich cream and cleanses the palate.

🌿

The Herb & Citrus Brightness Rule

Add lemon zest and fresh dill at the very end off heat. Cooking fresh herbs too long dulls their volatile aromatic oils; adding them last bursts with fresh garden brightness.

Culinary Execution

Step-by-Step Culinary Method

01

Prep & Dice the Salmon

Pat salmon fillets completely dry with paper towels to ensure a golden crust. Cut into 1-inch uniform cubes. Season generously with sea salt, freshly cracked black pepper, and half the lemon zest.

Chef Tip: Cold salmon cuts much cleaner into crisp uniform cubes than room-temperature fish.
⏱️ Prep: 5 mins
02

Boil the Penne Rigate Al Dente

Bring a large pot of heavily salted water (it should taste like the ocean) to a rolling boil. Drop the Penne Rigate and boil for **9–10 minutes** until *al dente* (firm to the bite). Reserve 1 cup (240ml) of starchy pasta water before draining!

⏱️ Cook: 10 mins
10:00
03

Golden Sear on Salmon Cubes

Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add salmon cubes in a single layer without overcrowding. Sear undisturbed for 2–3 minutes per side until light golden crisp. Transfer to a side plate (don't overcook—salmon cooks more in sauce!).

⏱️ Sear: 6 mins
06:00
04

Sauté Aromatics & Wine Deglaze

Reduce skillet heat to medium. Add remaining butter, diced shallot, and minced garlic. Sauté for 1–2 minutes until fragrant and translucent. Pour in dry white wine, scraping up all flavorful brown fond stuck to the bottom. Simmer 2 minutes until reduced by half.

⏱️ Deglaze: 4 mins
04:00
05

Build Velvety Cream Reduction & Emulsion

Pour in heavy whipping cream and bring to a gentle simmer for 3 minutes. Stir in grated Parmigiano-Reggiano until melted. Splash in 1/3 cup reserved pasta water, whisking vigorously until sauce coats the back of a spoon.

⏱️ Simmer: 4 mins
04:00
06

The Grand Combination & Garnish

Toss cooked penne pasta into the sauce. Add fresh baby spinach and stir for 1 minute until wilted. Gently fold in seared salmon cubes, chopped fresh dill, 2 tbsp lemon juice, and remaining lemon zest. Season with black pepper and serve immediately with extra parmesan!

🍽️ Serve Hot
